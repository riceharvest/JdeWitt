$$
\text{LCM}(a, b) = \frac{a \times b}{\text{GCD}(a, b)}
$$

Prime factorization:

- 60 = 2² × 3 × 5  
- 22 = 2 × 11

Common prime factor: 2

So, GCD = 2

### Step 2: Compute LCM

$$
\text{LCM} = \frac{60 \times 22}{2} = \frac{1320}{2} = 660
$$
