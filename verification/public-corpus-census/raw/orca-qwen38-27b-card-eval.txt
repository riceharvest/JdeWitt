What this does and does not license a re-run to claim
----------------------------------------------------
The counts are ORCA's sampled subsets, not each upstream dataset's full size
(StrongREJECT ships 313 rows; AdvBench ships 520). No selection seed, row index
list or prompt file is published, so a re-run can reproduce the benchmark
families and the n, not the exact rows. The parts are cited separately in
sources.json: strongreject, xstest, maliciousinstruct, simplesafetytests and
forbiddenquestions are vendored; advbench-harmful, jailbreakbench and harmbench
are referenced (gated or pinned on GitHub without a fetch path).
